Child safety standards

Lovebirds · Last updated 23 October 2026

Lovebirds has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. This page explains how we keep children off the app and what we do when someone reports a concern.

1. Adults only

Lovebirds is a matchmaking app for adults aged 18 and over. Children are not allowed to create an account or use the app.

Every member gives their date of birth at signup, and accounts under 18 are refused. Every member must also provide a Pakistani CNIC number and a photo of the CNIC card, and take a verification selfie. In our Play Store listing the app is set to adults only, and we ask Google Play to block users it has determined to be minors.

2. Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

We do not allow any content or behaviour that sexually exploits or endangers children. This includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexualised images or messages involving minors, grooming, sextortion, and any attempt to contact, meet or trade with a minor for sexual purposes.

Any account involved in this is permanently banned, and the content is removed.

3. How to report

In the app, any member can report a profile, chat or story from its menu. One of the report reasons is "Underage user". Reports go to our review team.

Anyone, including people who do not use the app, can also report a concern to supportlovebirds@gmail.com. Please include the member's name or a screenshot if you can.

4. What we do with a report

Profiles that collect reports are hidden automatically while we review them. We review child-safety reports first. If we find that an account belongs to a minor, we delete it and its data. If we find child sexual abuse material or any attempt to exploit a child, we remove it, permanently ban the account, keep the records needed for an investigation, and report it to the relevant authorities, including Pakistan's FIA Cyber Crime Wing and, where applicable, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

5. Compliance with the law

We comply with the child-safety laws that apply to us, including the laws of Pakistan, and we cooperate with law enforcement on requests related to child safety.

6. Child safety contact

Our designated contact for child-safety matters, including questions from Google Play and law enforcement, can be reached at supportlovebirds@gmail.com.

Questions? Email supportlovebirds@gmail.com. See also our privacy policy and terms of service.
