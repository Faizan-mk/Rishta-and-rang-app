Privacy policy

Rishta & Rang · Last updated 2 September 2026

Rishta & Rang is a matchmaking app for adults in Pakistan. This policy explains what we collect from you, why we need it, who else can see it, and how you get it deleted.

1. Who we are

Rishta & Rang ("we", "us") operates this app and is the data controller for the information described below. You can reach us at support@rishtaandrang.app about anything in this policy, including a request to see or delete your data.

2. What we collect

Account details: your email address and password (stored only as a salted hash — we never see it).

Profile details: your name, date of birth, gender, city, bio, what you are looking for, and the matrimonial fields you choose to fill in (religion, sect, education, family background, readiness).

Photos: the profile photos you upload.

Verification selfie: the selfie taken during signup, used to check a real person is behind the profile. It is never shown on your profile or to other members.

CNIC / ID: your CNIC number and, if you upload one, a photo of the card. Used only to check the format and to support a future identity review. Never shown to other members.

Location: the city you select. If you allow it, an approximate distance so nearby profiles can be ranked first. We do not track or store your precise location history.

Chat: the messages, voice notes, and photos you send in the app, and who you sent them to.

Usage: when you last opened the app, who you liked, who you blocked, and what you reported — the record safety review runs on.

3. Why we use it

To create and run your account, show your profile to other members, suggest matches, deliver your messages, apply your daily like limit and Explore+ entitlements, review reports and keep the community safe, and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your photos, selfie, CNIC, or messages for advertising, and we do not use them to train machine-learning models.

4. Your consent

You give us your profile, photos, selfie, and CNIC voluntarily, to be matched. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account. We keep a small amount of data after deletion only where the law or a live safety investigation requires it — see Retention.

5. Who else sees it

Other members see your profile: name, age, city, photos, bio, and the profile fields you filled in. They do not see your email, password, CNIC, verification selfie, or exact location.

Supabase is our data processor. Your account, profile, messages, and uploaded files are stored on Supabase infrastructure under a processing agreement; Supabase processes this data on our instructions and does not use it for its own purposes.

We disclose data to law enforcement only where we are legally required to, or where it is necessary to investigate a credible threat to someone's safety.

6. How long we keep it

Your account data stays while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, matches, and messages are deleted from our live systems, and encrypted backups roll off within 30 days.

Two exceptions: records connected to an open safety report or an account we banned are kept for up to 12 months so the same person cannot immediately return; and records the law requires us to retain are kept for the period the law specifies.

7. Your rights

You can see and correct your profile data at any time from Edit profile.

You can delete your account, and everything above with it, from Settings → Privacy & safety → Delete account. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

You can also ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or object to a specific use, by emailing support@rishtaandrang.app. We answer within 30 days.

8. How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed and never stored in readable form. Verification documents live in a private storage bucket that only you and our review process can read; profile photos live in a bucket that only signed-in members can read. Access is enforced by row-level security rules on every table, so one account cannot read another's messages or documents.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the relevant authority as required by law.

9. Adults only

Rishta & Rang is for people aged 18 and over. We ask for your date of birth at signup and refuse accounts under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we delete it and its data. If you believe a minor is using the app, email support@rishtaandrang.app.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top is always the version you are reading.

11. Contact us

Email support@rishtaandrang.app with any privacy question, data request, or complaint. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to your local data-protection authority.

Questions about either document? Email support@rishtaandrang.app.
